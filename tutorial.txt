
Thank you for trying out our game!

This is a game based around using commands found in linux to defeat enemies, and was created for hack.COMS 2026.

How to play:

Type commands into the terminal to fight enemies! You have 4 main actions: attack, heal, attack debuff, and attack buff. The commands to use these are given in cheatSheet.txt. 

Each round of combat, you get to execute 3 commands. 

You start with 10 total commands and have access to 5 of them at a time. 

When a command is used, it is moved to the discard pile. 

Commands that were usable and went unused on your turn are also sent to the discard pile at the end of your turn, giving a fresh batch of commands at the start of each turn. 

commands in the discard pile can’t be used again until all commands in the deck have been gone through.

Each enemy has a different set of commands they can use

At the end of your turn, use the exit command to let the enemy continue and have it cycle back to you.

After a battle, you receive new commands that can be used in future battles and are sent to the map.

Select the next level and keep going!
